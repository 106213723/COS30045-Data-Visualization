Exercise 4.7

Adding labels

A chart without labels is not very useful. This step names every bar and shows its exact value, which turns the shapes from 4.6 into a readable bar chart.

What this step does

The bars now start at x = 100, leaving room on the left for the brand names. Each bar and its labels are placed in one <g> group, and the group is moved down to its band with translate(0, yScale(d.brand)). Because the group sets the vertical position, the rectangle's own y is set back to 0.

Two <text> elements are appended to every group: the brand name, right-aligned with text-anchor: end just left of the bar, and the model count, placed just past the end of the bar with xScale(d.count).

Key idea

Grouping lets the bar and its labels move together: change one translate and all three shift as a unit. Try changing text-anchor from end to start or middle to see how the brand names line up.
