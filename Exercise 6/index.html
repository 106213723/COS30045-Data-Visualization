Exercise 6 · Interactive visualisations

Explore the data yourself

Interaction lets readers ask their own questions of a chart. Filter the histogram by screen technology, or hover over any television in the scatterplot to see its details.

Exercise 6.1 · Filtering

How much energy do televisions use?

Most televisions use less than 500 kWh a year (65% of models), with a long tail of large, power-hungry screens reaching over 2,600 kWh. Switch between technologies to compare their shapes: LCD models cluster lower, with a median of 265 kWh, while LED and OLED sit around 410 to 420 kWh.

Exercise 6.2 · Tooltips

Stars, size and technology together

Each dot is a television, coloured by its screen technology. Higher star ratings generally mean lower energy use, but hover over the dots high up the chart: they are almost all large screens. Use the legend to hide a technology and compare the others.

How the interaction works

01

Filtering with the update pattern

Each filter button re-bins the selected televisions and passes the new bins to the same bars with .data().join(). A transition animates each bar to its new height.

02

Tooltips from mouse events

mousemove on a dot fills a tooltip with that television's data and places it at the pointer using d3.pointer(); mouseleave hides it again.

03

Colour that always means the same thing

LED, LCD and OLED keep the same colours on every chart in the project, and the colours were checked to stay distinguishable for readers with colour-vision deficiency.
